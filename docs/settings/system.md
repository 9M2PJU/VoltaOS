# System

## 3D car

- **Load new model**: replaces the car with your own 3D model (`.glb`, `.gltf`, `.fbx`, `.obj`,
  `.dae`, `.blend`). Formats other than `.glb` are converted (it can take a minute). If the
  model is very heavy it warns you and offers **Lighten and load** or **Load as is**.
- **Repeat setup**: redoes the guided setup of **doors, hood, trunk, wheels and
  color**: it asks whether the model has openable parts and moves them one by one to recognize them;
  you can choose materials and meshes by hand and skip what the car does not have.
- In the car panel, **Car adjust** moves and scales the model (position, size, shadow, proportions,
  *Rotate 180°*) and the buttons on the car (lock, hood, trunk).

> 3D models have **licenses**: only use models you are free to use, and do not redistribute them in the repository.

## Performance

**Low performance mode**: removes the car reflection, panel blurs and other heavy
effects. Useful on less powerful computers.

## Language

Italian, English, Spanish, French, Portuguese. Changing it reloads the services.

## Connected services

**Guided setup**: connect or change maps, search, weather and Spotify step by step (see
[API keys](../api-keys.md)). **Open**.

## Keyboard

- **Virtual keyboard**: shows the on-screen keyboard when you tap a text field.
- **Keyboard languages**: the enabled languages appear in the menu of the globe icon 🌐 on the keyboard;
  at least one always stays active.
