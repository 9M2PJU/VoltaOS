# Audio

## Playback

- **Resume on startup**: at power-on the last track or the last radio station starts again.

## Devices

Shows the **outputs** (speakers) and **inputs** (microphone) detected by `pactl`. Tap
the row to choose the device ("Output device" / "Input device"). If nothing
shows up, check that `pactl` and PipeWire/PulseAudio are installed.

## Equalizer

It uses **EasyEffects** (to be installed): choose a **preset** from the list, or
*+ Create new preset…* to save the current equalization with a name (e.g. "Rock", "Deep
bass"); **Save** updates the selected one and **Delete** removes it. Without EasyEffects it shows
"Equalizer not available". The equalizer button of the music widget opens this entry directly.

## Balance

The cabin diagram: drag the **dot** to move the sound between left and right and between
front and rear.
