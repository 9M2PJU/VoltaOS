# Display and Lights

## Display

- **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Auto**. With *Auto*, *Automatic theme* opens: choose the time
  window in which it is dark ("Dark from … to …"); outside that window the theme is light.
- **Driving position**: **Left** or **Right**. With right-hand drive the interface is mirrored: the car
  panel and the apps swap sides, and the dock order is reversed.
- **Reduce blue light**: turns on the system's *Night light*, with warmer tones in the evening.
- **Brightness**: percentage slider. It is **simulated** with a veil over the window (the PC does not
  expose the real backlight).
- **Screen cleaning mode**: locks the touch for a few seconds while you clean the glass; to
  exit, press and hold.

## Lights

- **Ambient lights**: **Off**, **On** or **Night only** (in the same window as the automatic theme).
- **Ambient light color**: choice of the lights' color (and brightness).

> **Note.** The ambient lights are prepared for a Bluetooth LED strip (BLEDOM type): color,
> brightness and on/off are sent to a dedicated service. That service is **not included** in this
> version, so for now the page does not turn anything on.
