# CarPlay / Android Auto

- **Theme**: *Automatic* follows the interface's light/dark theme; it applies immediately.
- **Fit in the small panel** and **Fit in full screen**: the video is always 16:9.
  *Automatic* shows it whole with two bars; *Fill* stretches it to the edges (it distorts a little).
- **Resolution**: 480p, 720p or 1080p (the sharpest; the others are lighter on the computer). Android Auto
  only accepts these three sizes.
- **Density (DPI)**: *Auto* chooses based on the resolution (240 at 1080p). Lower = more content
  on screen but small text; higher = everything bigger.
- **Reconnect the phone**: resolution and DPI apply on reconnection, which starts by itself
  when you change them.
