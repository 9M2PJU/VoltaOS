# Apps and Arcade

## Apps

*Hide the apps you do not use*: they disappear from the drawer but stay installed, and you can always
bring them back from here. Each app has a switch. **Settings** cannot be hidden: it is the only
way to turn the others back on.

## Arcade

*Choose which ones to show in the Arcade.* Games you add in Bottles show up by themselves, with the
bottle name next to them ("Bottles · …").

For each game you can:

- **Choose the cover** (vertical image, e.g. 600×900) and the **hero image** (wide, for the big
  picture). Formats: PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP.
- **Reset** to go back to the automatic image.

See also [Arcade](../app/arcade.md).
