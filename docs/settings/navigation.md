# Navigation

- **Full-screen map**: the car panel disappears and the map takes its place: full
  screen when no app is open, blurred in the background when you open one.
- **Avoid tolls**: routes do not include toll roads.
- **Avoid ferries**: no ferry connections.
- **Map view**: **Heading** (the map rotates in the direction of travel) or **North** up.
- **Night effect (satellite)**: darkens the satellite view — **Off**, **On** or **Auto** (same
  window as the automatic theme).
- **GPS receiver**: shows whether a GPS is connected ("Connected: …"). Choose **Automatic** or,
  for a Bluetooth GPS, tap **Find Bluetooth GPS** and choose the device from the list of paired
  ones. With a USB GPS (u-blox) just plug it in.
- **Voice guidance volume**: slider for the voice volume. *The speech engine is not included in
  this version: the slider is prepared.*

See also [Map and navigation](../app/map.md).
