# Bluetooth (music from the phone)

The **Bluetooth** app is the fourth music source, next to YT Music, Spotify and Music: it plays the
**phone's** music through the PC's Bluetooth audio. To **pair** the phone use
[Settings › Bluetooth](../settings/bluetooth-network.md).

**Requirements:** `bluez`, a Bluetooth adapter, the paired phone.

## Screen

- **Devices**: the connected phone, with the **Connected** status and the track it is playing.
- **Connect phone / Connect another device**: leads to the Bluetooth settings.
- **Or play directly from these apps**: shortcuts to YT Music and Spotify, to listen
  to music from the car instead of streaming from the phone.
- Header like the other music apps: **unified search**, logo menu to switch apps,
  **unified queue**.

## Widget and controls

Title, artist and cover (when the phone sends them) appear in the music widget. With the
phone you can use play/pause, previous and next. **The phone does not report the upcoming
tracks**: the queue only shows what you add from YT Music, Spotify or Music, which starts after the
phone's track. Not all phones send title and artist: in that case the device name is shown.

## When the phone is not connected

The apps that need it (Phone, Messages) show a **"Connect your smartphone to
Bluetooth"** screen with a button to open the Bluetooth settings.
