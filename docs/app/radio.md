# Radio

Radio from **all over the world** via [Radio Browser](https://www.radio-browser.info), with high-resolution
logos. No key. A static list of Italian stations also works without the search network.

## How to use it

- **Choosing the country**: tap the country at the top and search (*Search a country…*): the stations update.
- **Stations**: list with logo and name; **Show more stations** loads more pages. Tap one
  to listen.
- **Search…**: by station name; results appear under "Results for: …".
- **Direct tuning**: buttons to tune a specific station.
- **Favorites**: the **heart** (also the one in the music widget) saves the station; the
  *Favorites* list stays on the PC.

## In the music widget

With the radio on air the widget shows the station's logo and name. The previous/next buttons
are dimmed and the play button becomes **stop** (a radio cannot be paused). The heart saves or
removes the station from favorites.

## Resume on startup

With *Settings › Audio › Resume on startup* on, the last radio starts again at power-on.
